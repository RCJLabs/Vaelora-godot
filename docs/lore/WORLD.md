# Vaelora: the world

Part of the lore bible (`LORE.md`). **Plain text** is what you decided; ***(suggested)*** is
what I filled in; **🔒** is what almost nobody in the world knows. Every name here is a
suggestion unless it's in `LORE.md` already.

---

## The continents

**Several continents, each with its own ruler.** *(suggested)*

| Continent | What it's like | Who rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Orveth** (the game's continent) | Temperate heartlands, river valleys, old forests, mountains in the north and east. The Greywater Vale is on its far northern edge | **The Ilunar Empire**: Empress Corvina, from **Ilunhall** |
| **Sundra**, across the southern sea | **A massive desert with numerous oases**, and the Glass Coast on its northern shore, where the Mage War burned the sand to glass | **The Oasis Thrones**: each oasis has a lord, under a **Sovereign of Wells** chosen among them |
| **The Scatter**, the northern and western seas | **Island tribes**, sea-roads, storms. **The Pale Gate** is out here somewhere | Many chiefs, and a **Sea-Queen** most of them will follow in a war |
| **The far west** | Nobody's sure. Ships go, and rarely come back | Unknown |

## Orveth's regions *(suggested names)*

- **Ilunhall, the capital.** Your "magnificent, beautiful capital, similar to Valhalla":
  - Great timbered halls with gilded roofs that climb a mountainside above a fjord.
  - Each hall is bigger than the last, up to the **Hall of Spears** at the top, where the
    Empress sits under a roof held up by a thousand spear-shafts.
  - Rune-lit bridges arch between the halls. Light-stone in the eaves makes the whole city
    glow gold at night, so it looks magical even after the Dimming.
  - Feasts in the great halls never quite stop.
  - 🔒 The light-stone is residue, and it's dimming. The city's glow is a little fainter every
    year, and the Empire pretends not to notice.
- **Ithmarren, the forest city.** The elves' city in the canopy of a forest of giant trees,
  with walkways, lantern-gardens and houses grown, not built. The great houses look down on
  everything, in every sense.
- **Karrowdeep, the city in the mountain.** Your "cave system in a mountain with a whole
  society". The Deepfolk's city fills a hollow mountain:
  - Terraced halls around a central chasm, lit by veins of rune-ore.
  - Markets on bridges over the dark.
  - The guilds' law carved into the walls.
  - Below the lowest gallery, the mines go down further than anyone admits.
- **The Riverlands**, your "river towns": the Greywater and its sister rivers, with walled
  river towns, barges, toll-forts and mills. **Riverwatch** is the northernmost. The **River
  Compact** runs the water.
- **The Grieving Fields:** the old battlefields of the Ashen Rising, grey grass and quiet
  villages. The Ashen live here.
- **The Hearthlands:** rolling hill country of inns, orchards and market towns, the small
  folk's home. (Every road in the Empire also has a small-folk inn on it.)
- **The Iron Moors:** the orc clan-holds and forge-towns of the high north-east, and the
  Empire's best soldiers.
- **The Thornwood:** the wild borderland forest north of the Greywater Vale (in the demo).

## The twelve sites

**The places where the Vael first set down from the Wanderer**, and **where the residue pools
deepest: magic's wells.** The stone circle in the Greywater Vale is one. *(suggested)* Most
have a stone circle or a college built on them, and the deepest few are the most fought-over
places in the world.

## The Pale Gate

**Out at sea, on an island that's never on the same map twice.** *(suggested)* It's somewhere
in the Scatter, north of Orveth. The island is wherever the Wanderer's light touches the sea
on the dark of the Wanderer, which is why the letter says "north to the Pale Gate by the next
dark of the Wanderer". Island tribes give it a wide berth. Aeviro's hut is on its shore.

## Everyday life

- **Money:** gold, silver and copper, struck by each realm. *(suggested)* The Empire's coins:
  the gold **crown**, the silver **mark**, the copper **bit**. Money in the game is "gold" for
  now.
- **Languages:** one common tongue, plus each people's own. *(suggested)* The common tongue is
  **Trade**; the Collegium writes its charters in **High Ilunar**, an old imperial tongue.
- **When people die:** nobody knows what happens, and every people argues about it.
  - **Burial:** burned, so nothing can drink from them; buried in barrows with grave-goods; or
    given to rivers. It differs by people (see `PEOPLES.md`).
- **Prayer:** something answers prayers at the shrines. It stays **a mystery for good**.
- **Wisps:** it depends on the people (see `PEOPLES.md`). Across the Empire, wisps are pests,
  good luck, a thing to frighten children with ("the lights will drink your breath"), and a
  crop that essence-hunters sell.

### The festival everyone keeps *(suggested)*

**Lanternfast**, on the dark of the Wanderer:
- Every window in the Empire has a lit lantern.
- Children sing "Hallam and the Hundred Lights" (see `HISTORY.md`).
- People eat outdoors and nobody sleeps before midnight.
- Each people says the lanterns are for something different:
  - **Humans:** "so the lights can't get in".
  - **Passengers:** "so the lost lights can find their way".
  - **Small folk:** "because it's a party".
  - **Ashen:** no lanterns, only silence.

🔒 Every lantern lit "to keep the lights out" is a tiny echo of the Hunt. The Passengers'
version is the true one.

## Creatures

- **Dragons: the Vael's great mounts, gone wild.** Rare and terrifying. *(suggested)* Only a
  few dozen live, mostly on Sundra's high mesas and in the Scatter. Some remember their riders.
- **Hollowed beasts:** grey and empty-eyed, like the hollow wolf.
- **Vael-creatures gone wild:** strange, beautiful and dangerous. *(suggested)* Examples:
  - **glassdeer**, whose antlers ring when they run;
  - **lantern moths** the size of a hand, which follow wisps;
  - **sky-rays**, livestock that once floated over Vael pastures, now drifting in herds over
    Sundra.
- **Guardians:** *(suggested)* the Vael brought guardians, and **the Stone Guardian in the Old
  Barrow is one**, still guarding what it was told to guard nine hundred years ago.
- **Things in the deep places the Deepfolk wake:** *(suggested)* the **blind choir**, pale
  things below Karrowdeep that sing in the dark. Nobody who's heard the song all the way
  through has described it.
- **Ordinary wildlife** where people live: wolves (the Greyfang), bears, boars, deer.
