# Vaelora: magic

Part of the lore bible (`LORE.md`). **Plain text** is what you decided; ***(suggested)*** is
what I filled in; **🔒** is what almost nobody in the world knows.

## The rules (decided)

- **Nine schools.** Not a copy of the Elder Scrolls; some are renamed, and two are Vaelora's own.
- **Every school can carry a whole playthrough on its own.** Focus on one and you can finish
  the game with it.
- **No magic at all is a full path too:** weapons, archery, armour and stealth.
- Ordinary magic draws on the Vael's **residue**, which is no longer anyone. Only wisps are
  still someone.
- **Every Wisp Essence is a three-way choice:**
  - **absorb it:** more power, and corruption;
  - **release it:** it goes to its rest;
  - **leave it:** it can be gathered with its people.
- **Flight:** common before the Dimming; now **only a handful of living masters can fly**.
- **The Collegium is several colleges, one per school, and they compete.**

## The nine schools *(names and designs suggested)*

Each entry gives what the school is, **how a player who uses only this school wins fights**,
and a few spells from novice to master. In the game today, three schools exist: **Ruin**
(currently called Destruction), **Mending** (Restoration) and **Shaping** (Alteration).
Renaming them is a display-text change; the save data keeps the old IDs.

### 1. Ruin *(was Destruction; in the game)*
Fire, frost and storm, thrown hard.
- **Solo play:** the straightforward blaster. Kill it before it reaches you, and use frost to
  slow what gets close.
- **Spells:** Flames and Firebolt (built), Frost Burst (built), chain lightning, a meteor at
  mastery.

### 2. Mending *(was Restoration; in the game)*
Healing, wards, and light that burns what's hollow.
- **Solo play:** the unkillable paladin. Out-sustain everything, ward the big hits, and
  **sear the hollowed and the corrupted**, which take heavy damage from Mending.
- **Spells:** Healing and Mending (built), Healing Ward (built), a searing light against the
  hollowed, and at mastery a sanctuary that heals you and harms the corrupted inside it.

### 3. Shaping *(was Alteration; in the game)*
Matter, weight and body: stone skin, light, force.
- **Solo play:** the battlefield shaper. Raise stone walls, shove enemies off ledges, harden
  your skin, and make the ground fight for you.
- **Spells:** Stoneflesh and Candlelight (built), telekinetic shove, stone wall, levitation,
  and at mastery **flight**.

### 4. Glamour *(Illusion, renamed)*
Light and minds: what people see, and what they believe.
- **Solo play:** your examples. **Make copies of yourself to confuse enemies** (decoys draw
  attacks), **trick a mind into fighting for you** (charm or frenzy), turn invisible to
  reposition, and send enemies fleeing in fear. You rarely deal damage yourself; your enemies
  do it to each other.
- **Spells:** mirror image (decoys), fear, frenzy (turn on its allies), charm (fights for you),
  and at mastery a mass charm of a whole camp.

### 5. Binding *(Conjuration, renamed)*
Calling things and holding them: summoning, binding, raising.
- **Solo play:** the commander. Fight beside called creatures, bind enemies in place, and at
  higher levels raise the fallen to fight for you.
- **Spells:** call a wolf-spirit, bind (root an enemy), summon a guardian, raise the dead.
- 🔒 **Horror:** what you "call" are echoes of Vael-creatures that remember their masters. At
  high corruption, some of them look at you as if they know what you are.

### 6. Runecraft *(Enchanting, made combat-ready)*
Runes, wards, and magic stored in objects.
- **Solo play:** the trapper and artificer. Set rune-traps (fire, frost, binding, alarm), fight
  from behind rune-wards, and wield weapons you enchanted yourself. You plan the battlefield
  before the fight starts. A natural partner for the Deepfolk.
- **Spells:** rune-trap, ward-circle, enchant weapon, and at mastery runes that recharge on
  their own.

### 7. Sight *(Divination)*
Seeing far, and seeing ahead.
- **Solo play:** the duellist-seer. See attacks a moment before they come (slowed time for
  perfect dodges and parries), mark weak points for huge hits, see through walls and
  invisibility, and curse enemies with bad luck.
- **Spells:** foresight (slow time briefly), weak point, true sight, ill fortune, and at mastery
  a few seconds in which you know everything that's about to happen.

### 8. Tidecraft *(one of Vaelora's own)*
The pull of the two moons: gravity, tides, the weight of the sky.
- **Solo play:** the moon-mage, **stronger or weaker by the moons**. The game already has a
  day/night clock and moon phases.
  - Pull enemies in, hurl them away, pin them to the ground, crush them.
  - Strongest when Ilun is full or the Wanderer is dark, weakest in between.
  - You plan your fights by the sky.
- **Spells:** pull, push, gravity well, moon-crush, and at mastery a tide that lifts
  everything in an area into the air.
- 🔒 It's the Wanderer's own pull: the Vael's ship, still reaching for its people.

### 9. Drawing *(Vaelora's own; forbidden)*
Taking life and power from living things.
- **Solo play:** the vampire mage. Drain health, stamina and magic from enemies to fuel
  yourself, and grow stronger as your corruption grows. It's the most powerful school, and
  the only one that corrupts you.
- **Spells:** draw (a life-drain beam), siphon magic, the Hollowing Touch, and at mastery
  drawing from every living thing nearby.
- **No college teaches it.** The Collegium bans it. The **Vaerd** practise it. You learn it
  from them, from forbidden books, or by drinking enough wisps that it comes to you on its own.
- 🔒 It's how the Hunt was done.
- (This is corruption option D, "the Draw spell", from the corruption draft, returning as a
  whole school. The demo doesn't need it.)

## The Collegium: eight colleges *(suggested)*

**Several colleges, one per school, competing (decided).** Drawing has none.

| College | School | Seat | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Ember College | Ruin | Ilunhall | Proud, loud, and the Empire's war-mages |
| The Lamp College | Mending | The Riverlands | Healers and hospitals; Magister Vessa trained here |
| The Anvil College | Shaping | Karrowdeep | Deepfolk-run builders and stone-shapers |
| The Veil College | Glamour | Ithmarren | Elven, secretive, and the best liars in the Empire |
| The Bell College | Binding | The Iron Moors | Orc-led; a summoned thing must be bound by oath |
| The Seal College | Runecraft | Karrowdeep | Rivals of the Anvil College, in the same mountain |
| The Glass College | Sight | The Glass Coast, Sundra | Seers in a tower of fused glass on the Mage War's scar |
| The Tide College | Tidecraft | An island in the Scatter | Sailors and stargazers; the closest to the Pale Gate, and the least welcome at court |

**Each college has its own questline, and they all lead to similar endings** (decided). *(suggested)*
Every college line ends at the same truth: its founders were hunters, and its school was taken
from the Vael. Each faces it differently.

**The rules nobody can explain** (decided): mage licences, and "never drink from a wisp". 🔒 The
founders' guilt survives as law.
