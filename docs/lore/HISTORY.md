# Vaelora: a history

Part of the lore bible (`LORE.md`). **Plain text** is what you decided; ***(suggested)*** is
what I filled in; **🔒** is what almost nobody in the world knows.

**Counting the years:** people count **years since the Arrival** (written "AA") and, in
ordinary speech, **rulers' reigns**: "the ninth year of Empress Corvina", or "900 AA".
*(suggested)* The game is set in **902 AA**.

## Timeline

| When (AA) | What happened | What people believe instead |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | 🔒 The **Wanderer**, the Vael's ship, appears in the sky as a new green moon. The Vael come down at **twelve places**. They're refugees: their own world has been destroyed. **The Passengers** come down with them | The Arrival: the lights came, and magic with them |
| **0–20** *(suggested)* | 🔒 **The wonder years.** The Vael, gentle and curious, live among Vaelora's peoples. Magic spreads from them. So do their creatures: companions, mounts (among them **the dragons**), livestock, guardians | A golden dawn, much sung about and little described |
| **c. 20–120** | 🔒 **The Hunt.** A slow century of killing, led by **a pact of kings and mages**, with **the founders of the Collegium** among them. The dead Vael become **wisps**. Some Vael fight back, and their creatures still remember how. Elves drink the most. Many of the hunters who drink too much **hollow**. Aeviro, a small-folk hunter, is one of them | Nothing: the Hunt isn't in any history |
| **c. 120** | 🔒 **The covering.** The Arrival story is invented to replace the Hunt, the records are burned, and the witnesses die off. The **small folk** swear off magic, and only the oath survives. *(suggested)* The pact's kings found the **Ilunar Empire**; its mages found the **Collegium** | The Collegium is founded "to study the gift" |
| **120–c. 600** | **The golden age of magic**, built on the residue the Vael left. *(suggested)* **Flight is common**: masters cross the Empire in a day. **Caelmere**, a city held in the air by magic, is built. The **twelve sites**, where the residue pools deepest, become magic's wells | The best of times. It is, for everyone but the wisps |
| **c. 340–360** *(suggested)* | **The Ashen Rising.** The **Ashen**, descended from the hollowed hunters and treated as cursed by the elven houses that cast out their ancestors, rise against them. A bitter war, ending in a peace neither side forgives | Elves: a rebellion of the cursed. Ashen: the war for our name |
| **c. 610** *(suggested)* | **The Mage War.** Two colleges fight over the deepest of the twelve wells. The land between them burns, and the scar never heals: **the Glass Coast**, where the sand ran to glass | A war of pride, the reason licences are strict |
| **c. 700–760** | **The Dimming.** The stones give less, and magic thins everywhere. *(suggested)* Flight fails. **Caelmere falls out of the sky** (**the fall of a great city**, 731 AA), killing tens of thousands. | **760–900** *(suggested)* | **The long thinning.** The Empire holds together, but its edges fray. The colleges compete over what magic is left. Hollowing outbreaks come and go, and **the Passengers are blamed**. Mage-hunters grow bold | Hard times, and someone to blame |
| **c. 880** *(suggested)* | 🔒 **Aeviro begins the gathering.** The **Vaerd** cult is founded among hollowing mages, promised a cure and a cause: releasing the Vael to their rest | — |
| **901–902** | **The game.** The stones of the Greywater Vale wake early, wisps fade, a hollow wolf walks out of the north, and The First is made | Small wrong things |

## The legend everyone knows *(suggested)*

**"Hallam and the Hundred Lights."** Every child in the Empire knows it, and it's sung at
Lanternfast:

> When the lights came down, they were many, and they were hungry, and they crept into the
> houses at night and drank the breath of sleeping children. Hallam the Lamplighter, a
> farmer's son, went out with a lantern and an iron hook, and he hunted the lights through
> a hundred nights, and caught one each night, and put it out. On the hundredth night the
> last light begged him for its life, and Hallam said, "You should have stayed in the sky."

🔒 Hallam was real. He was one of the pact's hunters, and the Vael he caught were not hungry.
The Empire's favourite children's story is a mass murderer's boast, and the "lights" in the
song are people. *(suggested)* Aeviro can sing every verse, and knew the man.
