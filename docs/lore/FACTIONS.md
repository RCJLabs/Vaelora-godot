# Vaelora: powers and guilds

Part of the lore bible (`LORE.md`). **Plain text** is what you decided; ***(suggested)*** is
what I filled in; **🔒** is what almost nobody in the world knows.

## The powers of the full game (decided: all eight)

| Power | What it is *(suggested where not in LORE.md)* |
|---|---|
| **The Ilunar Empire** | The crown, its legions and its nobles. Empress Corvina rules from Ilunhall. Its far edges, like the Greywater Vale, see little of it but the taxes. 🔒 Founded by the pact's kings |
| **The Collegium** | The eight colleges (see `MAGIC.md`). They license mages, and compete for students, wells and the Empress's ear |
| **The Mercy** | The cult *(suggested name, replacing "the Vaerd"; its members are "the merciful")*. Hollowing mages promised a cure and a cause. Run in the Greywater Vale by Magister Vessa ("V."), and secretly by Aeviro ("A.") |
| **The Thornwood Free**, and outlaws like them | The forest outlaws, and others like them across the Empire who say the law is the problem |
| **The River Compact** | The merchant league that runs the roads and rivers: tolls, barges, caravans, and the Riverlands' real government |
| **The Deep Guilds** | The Deepfolk mining guilds under guild-law, rich and frightened |
| **The Lantern Order** | The church-like order that hunts "corrupt" mages. Lantern-bearing inquisitors who burn essence-drinkers. 🔒 Its first Grand Lantern was Hallam's son, and its creed is the Hunt in a new coat |
| **The Choir** | The Passengers' hidden song-keepers, who know fragments of the truth and are afraid to sing them |

## Joinable guilds (decided: at least five, closer to seven, two of them "evil")

*(suggested)* Seven, each with its own questline.

| Guild | Kind | Its questline in a line |
|---|---|---|
| **The Legion** | The Empire's army | Rise from recruit to commander. You learn what the Empire is built on, and decide whether to defend it anyway |
| **The River Compact** | Merchants and caravans | Run a trade route, break a rival, and find out what the barges carry up the river at the dark of the Wanderer |
| **The Deep Guilds** | Crafters and miners | Earn a guild-mark in Karrowdeep, then follow the seam that whispers |
| **The Lantern Order** | Mage-hunters | Hunt "corrupt" mages, and find out how corrupt the Order is. Hard to stay in if you're corrupted yourself |
| **The Thornwood Free** | Outlaws and rebels | Steal from tax-carts, free prisoners, and decide whether the cause is worth what it costs |
| **The Lampless** *(evil)* | Thieves | They work on the dark of the Wanderer, when every window has a lantern and every eye is on the sky. Burglary, forgery, fencing, and one impossible heist in Ilunhall |
| **The Hush** *(evil)* | Assassins | A contract is a whisper left at a grave. Nobody has ever seen a member of the Hush speak. Murder for hire, and a ladder that ends at the Hall of Spears |

**Plus the eight college questlines** (decided: each college has its own, and they lead to
similar endings). **The Mercy** can be joined, or infiltrated, in the main quest. *(suggested)*
It isn't a guild, because it belongs to the main story.

Scope note: seven guilds and eight college lines is about fifteen questlines, roughly
Skyrim-sized. It's a full-game ambition: a few at a time, after the demo.
