# Starting Races

Three races for the vertical slice and early game. Each has a clear fantasy, mechanical lean, and signature ability. More races can be added later.

Visual target: stylized but grounded. Strong silhouettes, readable forms, atmospheric lighting, practical clothing/armor mixed with fantasy elements.

## Human

**Core fantasy:** The adaptable everyman/everywoman. Flexible and able to excel at almost anything through effort.

**Traits**
- Faster skill improvement through use (or an extra early trait/perk point).
- Mild bonus to reputation gains with most factions.

**Starting stat lean**
- Balanced across Strength, Endurance, Agility, Intelligence/Magicka, etc.

**Signature ability — Adaptable**
- Once per rest (or on a long cooldown): gain a temporary bonus to the next skill you use, or a small universal buff for a short time.

**Visual notes**
- Varied builds and features. Practical traveler or adventurer gear. Can lean martial, magical, or mixed.

## Elf

**Core fantasy:** Graceful, long-lived, and magically attuned. Strong in magic, precision, or stealth.

**Traits**
- Higher starting Magicka and/or spell effectiveness.
- Bonus to stealth, archery, or fine manipulation.
- Slightly lower physical toughness / Health.

**Starting stat lean**
- High Intelligence/Magicka and Agility.
- Lower Strength and Endurance.

**Signature ability — Innate Focus**
- Begins knowing one basic utility or offensive spell, **or** can briefly amplify magic power / reduce spell cost.

**Visual notes**
- Slender builds, elegant or nature-touched clothing and light armor. Distinct ear shape, refined features, possible subtle magical accents (runes, glowing eyes in certain lighting, etc.).

## Orc

**Core fantasy:** Powerful and resilient warriors. Direct, tough, and intimidating in combat.

**Traits**
- Higher starting Health and melee damage.
- Damage resistance, stagger resistance, or bonus when health is low.
- Penalty to stealth or pure magical aptitude.

**Starting stat lean**
- High Strength and Endurance.
- Lower Agility or Magicka.

**Signature ability — Battle Fury**
- Short-duration buff that increases melee damage and damage resistance (or unlocks a powerful charged attack). Cooldown-based.

**Visual notes**
- Broad, muscular builds. Tusks or pronounced lower canines, strong brow, practical heavy or medium armor mixed with tribal or clan markings. Weathered, grounded appearance rather than cartoonish.

---

These designs are intentionally lightweight so they can be prototyped quickly. Numbers, exact ability tuning, and deeper cultural lore can be expanded once the vertical slice is running.
