# Lore questionnaire

The goal is to find out what world **you** want, not to make you write one. Most questions
are multiple choice with examples; pick by gut feeling. Your answers become `LORE.md` (the
lore bible), then the demo's quests, dialogue and names.

**How to answer** (from your phone is fine):
- Reply with the number and letter(s): `4: B`, `9: A + a bit of C`, `12: D, but darker`.
- Mix options, change them, or write your own. "**?**" means *you decide, then show me*.
- Skip anything. Partial answers are fine, and you can answer in several messages.
- Sections 1 and 13 matter most. Start there if you only have five minutes.

---

**1. Which game, book or show worlds do you love?** List up to five, and in a few words

## What happens next

1. I turn your answers into `LORE.md`, filling every **?** and gap with a suggestion marked
   *(suggested)*. You approve, change, or strike.
2. Then the demo questline (3–5 main quests, 5–8 side quests) as an outline for you to approve.
3. Then the quests, dialogue and names go into the game, replacing the placeholders. Tests
   check that every quest can be finished.
